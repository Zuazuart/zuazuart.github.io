About the artist

Precision, contrast, and custom design.

Focused tattoo work for clients who want a piece designed with intention from the first message to the final session.

Zuazuart

Custom tattoo artist based in Madrid.

Specialized in blackwork, black and grey realism, and color realism.

My work is built around contrast, clean readability, and designs that fit the person wearing them. I keep the process focused so each client gets careful planning, honest guidance, and a strong final piece.

Every tattoo is custom-designed for the project. No templates, no rushed decisions, and no copy-paste approach.

I take a limited number of appointments each month so the design, preparation, and session all receive the attention they need.

Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I book?

Use the booking form and include your idea, placement, size, and references.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A non-refundable deposit is required to secure an appointment and goes toward the final price.

How long is the wait?

Availability depends on the project and current schedule. You will receive options after your request is reviewed.

Can I send my own design?

Yes. References are welcome, and I can use them to build a custom design that fits your body.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on size, detail, placement, and session length. A clear estimate is shared before booking.

Do you do cover-ups?

Sometimes. Send clear photos of the existing tattoo so I can assess what is possible.
