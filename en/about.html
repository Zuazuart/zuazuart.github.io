The approach

Tattoos with intention.

My work focuses on blackwork, black and grey realism and colour realism. Contrast and long-term readability guide the design.

References help define a direction. From there, we work on composition, size and placement for your project.

I work by appointment in Madrid’s Salamanca district. The exact address is shared when the appointment is confirmed.

Before we begin

Frequently asked questions.

How do I request an appointment?

Send your idea through the form. I will review the project and reply with the next steps.

Do I need to have a finished design?

No. You can send an idea and references so we can define the design direction together.

How is pricing calculated?

It depends on size, placement, detail and the sessions needed. The quote is confirmed before booking.

Does an inquiry confirm my booking?

No. The date is confirmed after reviewing the project and agreeing the deposit conditions.

Where is the studio?

In Madrid’s Salamanca district. You will receive the exact address when your appointment is confirmed.

Do you consider cover-ups?

They are considered case by case. Send clear photographs of the current tattoo so I can assess the options.
